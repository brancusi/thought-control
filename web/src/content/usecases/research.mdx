You're reading about replicated logs. Each paper gets a line in the journal the day you read it, with a `[[link]]` to the idea it touches: `[[Replay ordering]]`, `[[Tombstones]]`. Typing `[[` offers the pages you have; a new name makes the page when the line saves. You misspell one as `[[Tombstoens]]` and thc offers the page you meant before it makes a second one.

A week later you open `¶ Replay ordering`. Your own notes are at the top. Under them, *linked from* lists every journal line that mentions it, with its day. You didn't file anything; the links did.

For the long write-up you switch on Focus, and the screen is just the column of text, the date and a word count.

## The commands

Capture as you read:

```sh
$ thc add "Paper: CRDTs for outlines, see [[Replay ordering]] #paper"
added 2p7vh   Paper: CRDTs for outlines, see [[Replay ordering]] #paper  in today's journal
$ thc todo "Reread section 4 of the tombstones paper" -t paper --sched sat
```

Bring in an outline you drafted elsewhere:

```sh
$ thc import - --page "Replay ordering" < outline.md
imported 3 nodes under ¶ Replay ordering · tx SNK244 · thc undo --tx SNK244 reverts all of it
```

Find things:

```sh
$ thc search tombstones
$ thc show gcqh0                         # a page, its children and its backlinks
gcqh0   Replay ordering
  dp87y   Open questions
  linked from
    2p7vh   Paper: CRDTs for outlines, see [[Replay ordering]] #paper  in today's journal
$ thc q '#paper created>=-2w sort:date'
$ thc q 'ancestor:( text:Replay ) status:open'
```

Write:

```sh
$ thc p "Replay ordering"                # open the page to write in
```

Inside, a click on a link follows it (so does <kbd>⌃O</kbd> with the cursor in one), <kbd>⌃O</kbd> anywhere jumps to any page or day by name, and <kbd>⌥Z</kbd> toggles Focus. `:focus planner` adds a month calendar and the links beside the text.

## Why thc fits

- **Links do the filing.** Write where you are; the page collects its mentions on its own.
- **Plain text all the way down.** Paste Markdown in, copy Markdown out, and `thc export` writes a folder Obsidian can read.
- **Questions, not folders.** A query finds the open threads under a topic, or what you read in the last two weeks, without a tagging scheme decided in advance.
