`thc` with no arguments opens the app. Seven views sit along the top. Press <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>7</kbd>,
<kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⇧Tab</kbd> to step through them, or click a tab. The bottom row always
shows the keys that work right now. <kbd>F1</kbd> (or <kbd>?</kbd> outside writing) shows all
of them.

```
 [•] personal   Today   Inbox 2   Tasks   Pages   Journal   Search   Log 1
```

## 1 · Today

**What needs you now**, from every page, every day and every vault:

- **Overdue** (red)
- **Today**: due, scheduled, or a reminder firing
- **Doing**
- **Next 7 days**
- **Done today**

| Do | Key |
|---|---|
| Mark done | <kbd>x</kbd> |
| Change the due / scheduled date | <kbd>d</kbd> / <kbd>s</kbd> |
| Set the priority | <kbd>p</kbd>, then <kbd>h</kbd> <kbd>m</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> |
| Tag | <kbd>#</kbd> |
| Move it somewhere | <kbd>m</kbd> |
| Open it where it lives | <kbd>Enter</kbd> |
| Add something | <kbd>a</kbd> |
| Today ⇄ agenda (a week, by day) | <kbd>w</kbd> |
| The day or week before / after | <kbd>[</kbd> <kbd>]</kbd> / <kbd>&#123;</kbd> <kbd>&#125;</kbd> |
| Snooze or acknowledge a reminder | <kbd>z</kbd> / <kbd>Z</kbd> |
| Choose which vaults it shows | <kbd>*</kbd>, or click the scope in the header |
| See how it's built | <kbd>?</kbd> |

When a reminder fires while you're in `thc`, a toast offers `x done  z snooze  Z ack`. From the
shell, `thc today` and `thc agenda --days 7` print the same thing, and `--why` says why each
row is there (`← scheduled · repeating`).

<TuiFrame name="today" caption="Today: what's due now, then the next seven days, then what you finished." />

**Across vaults, every line says where it's from.** With more than one vault, each row ends with
its vault's name in that vault's colour, and the header says which vaults Today covers
(`Today · all vaults (3)`). Press <kbd>*</kbd> to choose: all vaults, just this one, or a checkbox
per vault. The choice is for this device; <kbd>s</kbd> in the picker makes it the default
everywhere.

<TuiFrame name="vaults" caption="Today across three vaults: each line ends with its vault, in its colour." />

<TuiFrame name="scope" caption="* chooses which vaults Today shows on this device." />

**Every view explains itself.** <kbd>?</kbd> on Today, Inbox, Tasks or a saved view shows how
it's built: each section, how many it holds, its query, and what that means with the dates
filled in. <kbd>e</kbd> edits it in your editor (as `Title · query` lines), <kbd>c</kbd> copies it
as a new view, and <kbd>r</kbd> puts a built-in one back. <kbd>?</kbd> again shows the keys.

<TuiFrame name="recipe" caption="? on Today: each section with its count, its query, and what it means with the dates filled in." />

**Lists keep their order.** Clicking, opening and coming back never reshuffle a list. A task
you finish stays where it is, dimmed, until you come back to the view (or press <kbd>⌃L</kbd>),
and something an agent adds appears without moving your selection. Only a sort change
reorders on screen.

## 2 · Inbox

Things captured without a home: from `thc add --inbox`, from agents, or from the `drop/` folder
in your vault (save a `.md` or `.txt` file there from your phone, and each paragraph or list
item becomes an inbox item). Triage each
one with <kbd>m</kbd> (move to a page or day), <kbd>t</kbd> (make it a task), <kbd>d</kbd> (give
it a date), <kbd>x</kbd> (done) or <kbd>D</kbd> (delete). The cursor moves on after each, so
triage is fast.

## 3 · Tasks

Every open task, filtered by a query. Press <kbd>f</kbd> (or <kbd>/</kbd>) and type, for example
`status:open #work due<=+7d sort:due`. Saved views sit on a row under <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd>.
At 120 columns the list becomes a table with due, scheduled, priority, where and who. Press
<kbd>,</kbd> to change the sort. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the filter without wiping your query.

The language is the same everywhere: here, in `thc q`, and in saved views. See
[Query language](../query-language/).

<TuiFrame name="tasks" caption="Tasks, filtered by a query, with saved views on the row above." />

## 4 · Pages

Your pages, with how many open items each has. Type to find one; <kbd>Enter</kbd> (or
<kbd>→</kbd>, or a double-click) opens it in the editor. Pages are made by linking
(`[[Name]]`) or from the finder (`+ new page`).

<kbd>4</kbd> always shows this list, never a page. The cursor rests on the page you opened last,
so <kbd>4</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> reopens it, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> in a page comes back here.

**Back and forward.** <kbd>⌘[</kbd> and <kbd>⌘]</kbd> (or <kbd>⌃⌥←</kbd> / <kbd>⌃⌥→</kbd> in any terminal) move
through the places you've been, like a browser: pages, days, views and vaults, with the cursor
where you left it. `:history` lists them, and a click goes there. <kbd>Esc</kbd> is different: it
goes **up**, to where you started (after following three links, <kbd>Esc</kbd> returns to the list
or day you began on, while <kbd>⌘[</kbd> goes to the page before).

In a terminal 120 columns or wider, a page has a **rail** of your pages on its left (recent
first, then A–Z, with open counts): click one to switch. A journal day gets a rail of days.
Narrower, a crumb (`¶ Pages › Health`) sits above the title instead. `page_rail = false` under
`[tui]` turns the rail off.

<TuiFrame name="pages" caption="The pages finder: type to narrow, Enter to open." />

## 5 · Journal

One page per day, opened as a document you write in (see [Writing](../writing/)). The strip
under the date jumps between days: <kbd>⌃P</kbd> / <kbd>⌃N</kbd>, or click a day. **Also
today**, at the bottom, shows what's due today from other pages.

## 6 · Search

Full text across pages, tags and notes, as you type. <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the match in
context. From the shell: `thc search "lisbon deposit"`.

<TuiFrame name="search" caption="Search: every page, tag and note, as you type." />

## 7 · Log

Every change, newest first: who made it (`you`, `◆ claude`), when, and what. <kbd>u</kbd> undoes
the selected change, as a new change: nothing is erased. <kbd>@</kbd> cycles whose changes you
see, and <kbd>R</kbd> rewinds a note to how it was.

<kbd>r</kbd> switches to the **review lane**, where agents' changes wait for you: <kbd>a</kbd>
accept, <kbd>u</kbd> undo, <kbd>A</kbd> accept all. See [Agents](../agents/).

<TuiFrame name="log" caption="The Log: every change, who made it, and one key to undo it." />

## Views with sections

Today, Agenda, Inbox and Tasks are **built-in views made of sections**, each section a query.
`thc view ls` lists them with your own. Today, as shipped:

| Section | Query |
|---|---|
| Overdue | `is:overdue status:open` |
| Today | `(sched<=today or due=today or is:alert) status:open -is:overdue` |
| Doing | `status:doing` |
| Next 7 days | `happens<=+7d happens>today status:open` |
| Done today | `done>=today` |

Make it yours. Sections are given in order, and replace the old ones:

```sh
$ thc view set today --section Due 'due<=today status:open' \
                     --section Waiting 'status:waiting'
$ thc view reset today              # back to the built-in
```

Or keep Today and make another:

```sh
$ thc view copy today acme-today
$ thc view set acme-today --scope vault:acme
$ thc q @acme-today
```

`--scope` sets the vaults every section reads (`vault:*`, `vault:(a or b)`). Today and Agenda
read every vault by default; see [Vaults](../vaults/).

**Plain saved views** are one query with a name:

```sh
$ thc view add work 'status:open #work sort:due' --tasks 1   # also slot 1 on the Tasks row
$ thc q @work due<=+3d                                       # views compose with more terms
$ thc view rm work                                           # thc undo brings it back
```

Views sync like notes, and undo like any write.

## Around the edges

- **The detail pane** (<kbd>&#92;</kbd>, at 120+ columns): everything about the selected item, plus
  its history and links.
- **Contexts** (<kbd>C</kbd>, or `thc context work`): show only what matches a saved view, for
  example only `#work` while you're at work. It's always named in the header while it's on, so
  nothing is ever hidden without you knowing. It's local to this device and never synced.
- **Vaults** (<kbd>V</kbd>): switch between [vaults](../vaults/), each with its own colour.
- **Focus** (<kbd>⌥Z</kbd> while writing): hides everything but your words. Choose what stays with
  `:focus`.
- **The leader** (<kbd>Space</kbd>, in any list): a panel shows every command, grouped by what
  it does. <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>g</kbd> go, <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>f</kbd> find,
  <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>n</kbd> new, <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>t</kbd> toggle,
  <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>v</kbd> saved views. You never need to remember a key: press
  <kbd>Space</kbd> and read. <kbd>⌫</kbd> steps back, <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes. While writing,
  Space is just a space.
- **Help** (<kbd>F1</kbd>, or <kbd>?</kbd> in lists): the keys for exactly where you are,
  grouped. <kbd>?</kbd> <kbd>?</kbd> shows every key everywhere.
- **The palette** (<kbd>:</kbd>, or <kbd>space</kbd> <kbd>space</kbd>): every command by name,
  with its key. Type a few letters and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

<TuiFrame name="leader" caption="Space opens the leader: every command, grouped, one letter each." />

When two devices changed the same note before they synced, the header says so
(`≠ 2 conflicts`). <kbd>c</kbd> on the row compares the two versions; you choose.
